QingYue OCR Droid Sans Regular is a modified font made from Android Open Source Project
DroidSansFallback.ttf and DroidSansMono.ttf (Apache License 2.0).

Modifications: scaled DroidSansMono glyphs from 2048 to 256 units per em; appended
their glyphs, metrics, and missing Unicode mappings to DroidSansFallback; renamed
the font family and PostScript names. Generated with fontTools 4.65.0. The exact
source hashes, generation script, and final hash are recorded in
third-party/ocr/FONT-PROVENANCE.md.

The original NOTICE and complete Apache License 2.0 text are in
DroidSansFallback-AOSP-NOTICE.txt.

Web-only additional modification (2026-09-23): removed inconsistent vhea/vmtx vertical metric tables and recomputed SFNT checksums. Glyphs and horizontal metrics are preserved. See THIRD-PARTY.md and font-preparation-reference.cjs in the source package. Android font unchanged.
